Meet the Candidate

A Statesman From the Bottom of Everything

All biography below is invented QuarkForce lore. Any resemblance to a qualified public servant is a happy accident.

Biography

Nobody knows precisely when Mayor of the Lake arrived. Local legend says he was already seated in the chair when the cave was discovered, and that the cave then politely rearranged itself around him. He has never been seen to stand, which supporters describe as "a stable platform" and critics describe as "a chair problem."

He emerged into public life when he noticed the lake had no one speaking for it. He found this strange, since the lake is the loudest thing in the region at three in the morning. He accepted the role of Mayor immediately, partly out of duty and partly because the sash was already there.

Political Philosophy

Government, he teaches, should work like groundwater: unseen, essential, and slightly alarming when it rises in the basement. His doctrine, Bottom-Up Governance, holds that every great decision begins somewhere dark and cold and must be carried upward by people who are willing to get wet.

His Relationship With the Lake

Complicated, devoted, and mostly one-sided in a way he insists is "mutual." He refers to the lake as "my constituency, my colleague, and my landlord." The lake has not commented, though ripples have been noted at key moments in his speeches.

Personal Values

Punctuality (tides are punctual), plain speech (echoes encourage it), and the sacred principle that nobody should be left alone in the dark unless they specifically asked for that.

Vision for the Future

A lake region where every cave has a lease, every creature has a hearing, and every citizen knows what is happening beneath the surface. He also wants a better boat.

Origin Story QuarkForce lore

In the QuarkForce universe, the Mayor is the last elected voice of the Lower Waterline, a district that formally dissolved beneath the surface long ago and forgot to tell him. He kept holding office anyway. The Staff of Standing Orders, topped with a tattered banner of uncertain nationality, is said to have chosen him. Or he found it. Accounts differ, and he refuses to settle them in public.

Oddly Specific Qualifications

  • Has pointed at the correct direction on every occasion he has pointed.
  • Holds the unofficial record for longest continuous staring contest with a sunset.
  • Fluent in echo, with working knowledge of reverb.
  • Has attended every meeting held in his cave, including the ones nobody invited him to.
  • Owns exactly one sash, worn daily, never laundered, "seasoned."
  • Maintains a perfect record of never having been drained.
  • Can identify a lake by smell, with a margin of error he declines to disclose.

Leadership Principles

  1. Listen from the bottom.
  2. Speak only when the acoustics are right.
  3. Never trust a surface you haven't leaned on.
  4. Hold the staff firmly; hold opinions loosely.
  5. No lake left behind.
